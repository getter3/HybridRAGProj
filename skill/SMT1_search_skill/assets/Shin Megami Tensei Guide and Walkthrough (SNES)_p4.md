## Guide and Walkthrough (SNES) by InfinityDragon

**Version:** 1.70 | **Updated:** 06/02/2013

## WALKTHROUGH (Continued)

#### Dealing with Echidna

Your main objective here, again, depends on your alignment. Chaos path players are here to report the death of Haniel and support the Gaian leader. Law path players, on the otherhand, are here to stop the Gaian leader from interrupting progress on the construction of the Cathedral. Neutral players are here to keep either side from gaining an upper hand.

Return to Echidna's room. Before entering, prepare your party for a boss fight if you are Law or Neutral aligned. Enter to room to speak with Echidna again. The interaction, again, depends on your alignment:

###### Law Path

Since you agreed to help the Law Hero, Echidna will recognize that you are not here to assist her, despite what the Chaos Hero told her. As punishment, she decides to tear you limb from limb. Continue the walkthrough below at the [Vile Echidna](#section315) boss section.

###### Chaos Path

Echidna congratulates you on defeating Haniel and tells you that the Gaians are about to mount an assault on the Great Cathedral itself. She then asks if you would be willing to lend your aid to the Gaian effort in preventing the Messians from summoning God. Since you are following the Chaos Path, agree to her request. You will gain a **massive** shift towards Chaos, and be semi-locked into it.

Echidna then creates a bridge from Tokyo Destiny Land to the Great Cathedral. Before doing that, however, you will want to fully lock in to the Chaos Path by being baptized into the Gaian Cult. Pick up this walkthrough at the [Baptism of Faith](#section323) chapter.

###### Neutral Path

Echidna congratulates you on defeating Haniel and tells you that the Gaians are about to mount an assault on the Great Cathedral itself. She then asks if you would be willing to lend your aid to the Gaian effort in preveting the Messians from summoning God. Since you don't want to assist one side over the other, decline her request for help. An enraged Echidna decides that you aren't obeying her commands, and in punishment will rend you limb from limb. Continue the walkthrough below at the [Vile Echidna](#section315) boss section.

##### Vile Echidna

Level: 81

HP: 1920

MP: 200

---

EXP: 1296

£: 3888

MAG: 1620

ITEM: Soma

---

Abilities:

Attack (~130 Physical damage, hits 2-4 characters)

Paralysis Bite (~165 Physical damage, inflicts Paralysis at ~33%)

Gas Breath (~35 Poison damage, inflicts Poison, hits 2-3)

Maha-Bufula (~50 Ice MT damage, inflicts Freeze at 95%)

---

Strategy: Echidna is pretty pathetic. Paralysis Bite is her best attack, but she only uses it once in a blue moon. Her physical attack isn't bad, but intelligent use of Me-Dia and Diarahan is more than enough to handle it. As for dealing with her, have the Main Hero use Sword attacks, and have your demons use their Attack command or their Extra if it is physical. Magic is pretty ineffective against Echidna, as she immunes many elements, and reduces the damage of the rest. Echinda is immune to normal Gun damage, but Magic Shells deal Charm damage, which Echidna only halves; it's still better to use Sword attacks as your Gun attack with Magic Shells will only deal 30-40 damage.

Since Echidna lacks any real effective status effects, just pressure her with damage and healing, and you should be fine.

---

After defeating Echidna, your path to the Great Cathedral differs depending on if you are Law or Neutral aligned.

###### Law Path

Law path players will want to return to the Law Hero to report the death of Echidna. Return to the Law Hero's room in Shinagawa to give the news to the Law Hero. He'll be overjoyed at your success and call in the current overseer of the Messian Church at Shinagawa, the Angel Haniel. Haniel will thank you for defeating Echidna and ask you if you want to continue working in the service of God. Answer yes, since you are following the Law path. You will gain a **massive** shift towards the Law alignment and be semi-locked in to it.

Haniel will grant you access to the Great Cathedral by creating a bridge to the east of Shinagawa. Before doing that, however, you will want to fully lock in to the Law Path by being baptized into the Messian Church. Pick up this walkthrough at the [Baptism of Faith](#section323) chapter.

###### Neutral Path

With Haniel and Echidna both dead, it may seem that all doors to the Great Cathedral have been shut. Not quite. If you did any exploring way back in Ginza, you may have found the old man from Inogashira Park, who said to visit him if all other paths seemed closed. Well, this would seem to be such a time, wouldn't you say?

Continue reading the walkthrough below at the next section: [The Path Less Travelled - Neutral Path Only](#section320).

#### The Path Less Travelled - Neutral Path Only

Since you've slain the two leaders who have direct access to the Great Cathedral, you'll need to find an alternative entrance. Toraport to a Terminal, then Transmit to Ginza. In Ginza, you want first head to [Ginza - Basement Two](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/snes/564896-shin-megami-tensei/faqs/52976?page=2#section224), then take the stairs at reference point **B3** to Basement Three.

This path is relatively short, overall. Starting from the stairwell to Ginza (reference point **GX**), head on over to reference point **OM** to speak with the old man from Inogashira Park. He'll congratulate you for being able to maintain such a narrow path of neutrality. He'll further state that regardless of who wins between the Messians and Gaians, humanity will suffer. To that end, he opens up the path behind him to the Great Cathedral so that you may put an end to the conflict.

**B3:** Follow the path east behind the old man to a stairwell (reference point **B2-1**), and take it to Basement 2-1.

**B2-1:** Follow the path east, ignoring the alcove to the north, then the alcove to the south. Head up another stairwell (reference point **B1**) to Basement One.

**B1:** First, pick up the Magic Incense at the reference point **MI**, then continue along to reference point **Sa** to collect a Sapphire. The head to the stairwell at reference point **B2-2** to head down to Basement Two (again).

**B2-2:** Follow the path southwest to the stairwell, and head up the stairs at reference point **CX** to the Great Cathedral.

**The Great Cathedral B1:** You are now in the Great Cathedral. You can use the map at [The Great Cathedral - First Floor/Basement One, Part One](#section415) section for reference if you wish. Follow the long corridor until you hear the voice from the Law and Chaos Heroes to your left and right. Talk to both the Law Hero (right/south) and the Chaos Hero (left/north) to learn about the Shiten-Ou, the Four Heavenly Kings who protect Tokyo.

Unlike the other two paths, you don't have a Baptism to participate in (at least not yet...). As such, skip the [Baptism of Faith](#section323) chapter below and pick up the walkthrough again with [The Four Heavenly Kings](#section336) chapter.

### Baptism of Faith

So you've killed either Haniel or Echidna, a massive boon to your respective allies, but you've actually never been formally indoctrinated into the tenets of the group you are aiding. It's high time you corrected that deficiency.

Law path players will want to read the [Messian Baptism](#section324) section below.

Chaos path players will want to skip down to the [Gaian Baptism](#section328) section and begin reading from there.

As mentioned, Neutral players do not have a baptism, and as such should continue the main walkthrough at [The Four Heavenly Kings](#section336) chapter.

#### Messian Baptism

First things first, if you're using the English translation patch and using an SNES emulator, you'll need to make a save state here. Load up a clean, \*unpatched\*, ROM and load that save state. Now return to the room where you talked to Law Hero and Haniel, and you'll see an old man. He'll ask you if you want to be baptised into the Messian Faith. Since the text is either gibberish or Japanese, you'll want to chose the top choice (which is "yes"). After the baptism, create another save state and load the English patched ROM back up.

###### Note

What happens if you don't load a clean ROM? Haniel shows up during the baptism, annoyed that you're too Chaotic and you end up killing Haniel, ruining any chance of doing the baptism. This is clearly a bug of some sort caused by the English patch, as he'll attack you even if you're pure Law.

After completing the baptism, you'll need to get your Main Hero's level up to 65. Hopefully you're in the low 60s already, so this shouldn't take \*too\* long. The best places to gain experience are Tokyo Destiny Land or the Four Mansions. Either warp to Ikebukuro and go to the city map to reach the North Mansion, or warp directly to T.D.L. I prefer the Mansions, simply because Succubi are incredibly annoying and slow the process down.

After reaching level 65, return to Shinagawa and go back to main chamber on the third floor. The Head Priest will tell you that you've become a great warrior for God and that some heavenly armor has been sent down to aid you. The sealed doors on this floor are now open, allowing you to collect this set of armor.

Exit the main chamber, then head east through the door to the long hallway. Go south one space, then turn west and head through the door, then go past the metal door. Continue down the short hall to find a chest containing the Jesus Gloves.

Exit back to the north-south hallway, then turn north and go through the one-way door. Turn east and go forward to the first door on the south wall. Continue past the second metal door and continue south to the chest containing the Jesus Armor.

Exit back to the east-west hallway, then turn west and go through the one-way door. Turn south and go forward to the first door on the east wall. Turn east and go through the door, past the metal door, and to the chest to collect the Jesus Helm.

Exit back to the north-south hall, then turn south and head through the one-way door. Turn east and go forward to the first door on the north wall. Turn north and go through the door and through the final metal door to the chest to find the Jesus Leggings.

Now that we're fully decked out in our holy armor (why couldn't they have called it Armor of Faith instead of Jesus armor?), it's time to check out the Cathedral. Exit Shinagawa, and you'll be back on the city map.

From the city map, head south from Shinagawa, then east around the purple tube. Once clear of the tube, head north to the bridge, then cross the bridge to the east. On the other side of the bridge, you will see a massive white building. This is the Great Cathedral...no wonder why it took so many workers so long to build this thing. Now you just need to find an entrance to enter this massive monument. From the bridge, head south-east and you'll come to a yellow building. Enter the yellow building, and you'll find yourself in the golden halls of the Cathedral.

#### Great Cathedral - Messian Headquarters

Head due north from the entryway into a hallway and you'll meet the Law Hero again. It appears there is more interference preventing the creation of the 1000 Year Kingdom. This time, it is due to four demons who protect Tokyo with some sort of barrier.

Before that, let's do some shopping. First, it's time to get the best guns money can buy. From the entrance to the Cathedral, go north two spaces, then turn west and go forward all the way to the wall. Turn north and go through the door to the Weapon shop. Purchase two Rail Guns--the insane costs of these guns is why I didn't have you buy any new weapons or armor since Ikebukuro. Hopefully you have 500-600k L right now, so picking up two Rail Guns shouldn't be hard. Now, you have a choice of what Ammo to use, depending on your personal playstyle. Magic Shells have the best status effect, but the lowest attack power, Medusa Shells offer the best balance between status effects (Stone) and damage, while Happy Shells have horrible status (Happy is useless) but the best damage. After you're through picking up weapons and any new ammo, exit the store.

From the weapon store, go east a space then north though the door to the Armor shop. Pick up a Sturm Helm and Sturm Leggings and equip them on the Heroine.

Save your game at the nearby Terminal so you can easily return to the Cathedral later.

With your new equipment, it's time to take on the Shiten-Ou, the four demons who are protecting Tokyo. Pick up the walkthrough again at [The Four Heavenly Kings](#section336).

As you may have learned from your explorations, Ueno is the location of the main Gaian Temple. Your objective here is to meet with the Gaian High Priest, be baptised into the Gaian Church, and collect the ultimate set of armor for the Chaos Path: the Tenma gear.

After completing the baptism, you'll need to get your Main Hero's level up to 65. Hopefully you're in the low 60s already, so this shouldn't take \*too\* long. The best places to gain experience are Tokyo Destiny Land or the Four Mansions. Either warp to Ikebukuro and go to the city map to reach the North Mansion, or warp directly to T.D.L. I prefer the Mansions, simply because Succubi are incredibly annoying and slow the process down. Once you are level 65, return to the Gaian High Priest.

This is another area where the translation patch will glitch. If you have the patch installed, the Priest will do nothing more than complement you for speading Chaos. If you load an unpatched ROM, then enter the High Priest's room, the Priest will open up the sealed door on the Second Floor, allowing you access to the Tenma armor set. After leaving the Priest's room, it is safe to save your game and load the patch back up.

Make your way to the Second Floor and enter the rooms at reference points **Gr**, **Ga**, **Ar**, and **He** to pick up the Tenma Greaves, Gauntlets, Armor, and Helmet, respectively.

Now that we're fully decked out in your demon armor, it's time to check out the Cathedral. Use the Terminal to transmit to T.D.L. and exit, and you'll be back on the city map. From T.D.L. head south across the bridge Echidna created to reach the Cathedral island. Enter the yellow building near the Cathedral to enter it.

#### Great Cathedral - Gaian Headquarters

Head due south from the Cathedral entrance and you'll run into the Chaos Hero. He'll tell you about the Shiten-Ou, the Four Heavenly Kings and that their power is needed elsewhere. Your next objective is to meet and recruit these characters.

Before that, let's do some shopping. First, it's time to get the best guns money can buy. From the entrance to the Cathedral, go due west, passing a random Gaian, to a wall. Go north a space, then east a space to find the Weapon shop. Purchase two Rail Guns--the insane costs of these guns is why I didn't have you buy any new weapons or armor since Ikebukuro. Hopefully you have 500-600k L right now, so picking up two Rail Guns shouldn't be hard. Now, you have a choice of what Ammo to use, depending on your personal playstyle. Magic Shells have the best status effect, but the lowest attack power, Medusa Shells offer the best balance between status effects (Stone) and damage, while Happy Shells have horrible status (Happy is useless) but the best damage. My preference is to keep the Magic Shells, since Charmed enemies will more than make up for the slight damage difference. After you're through picking up weapons and any new ammo, exit the store.

From the weapon store, go west a space, the south to the Gaia chapel. Shift one space east, then south through the door to the Armor shop. Pick up a Sturm Helm and Sturm Leggings and equip them on the Heroine.

Save your game at the nearby Terminal so you can easily return to the Cathedral later.

With your new equipment, it's time to meet on the Shiten-Ou, the four demons who are protecting Tokyo. Continue the walkthrough below at [The Four Heavenly Kings](#section336).

###### IMPORTANT

As with the end of the Thorman/Goutou arc earlier, you are about to enter into another Point of No Return.

Before returning to the Cathedral to report your success to the Law and/or Chaos Hero, make sure you've done absolutely everything you want to do. Once you return to the Law Hero, a point of no return event occurs. If you are on the Law or Chaos path, be certain that you have the Jesus or Tenma set of equipment. Also check the Optional Sidequests section to make sure you've done all available side-quests that you want.

After heeding the above advice and doing everything you want to, return to the Law or Chaos Hero. After talking to them, you will hear a rumble, and they will ask you to go outside to check out what happened. Do so and you'll see that Tokyo has been flooded.

Your alignment is now locked in at this point (sort of, see the [Frequently Asked Questions](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/snes/564896-shin-megami-tensei/faqs/52976?page=0#section29) section for common glitches/pitfalls). Return to the Law and/or Chaos Hero and they will comment on the flooding. Additionally, they will tell you that Ravana and Vishnu, longtime enemies, have renewed their feud in the old Tokyo Government Offices.

If you are on the Law path, the Law Hero will direct you to aid Vishnu and defeat Ravana. Likewise, if you are on the Chaos path, the Chaos Hero will direct you to aid Ravana and defeat Vishnu. If you are on the Neutral path, then each Hero will ask you to aid his respective ally and defeat his respective enemy.

Regardless of your path, the first order of business is finding a means of transportation to traverse the sea. Unfortunately, each path enters the Cathedral at a different entrance, so each path has a different means of acquiring sea transportation. Read the text box for your corresponding alignment below. You should utilize the maps for the Great Cathedral First Floor and Basement One in the The Great Cathedral, Part One (B1-1F) section to aid you.

###### Law Path

While in the south portion of the Cathedral (the Messian Encampment), head towards reference point **O1**. Along the way you'll meet a Messian who is about to sacrifice a monster because it is an evil demon. Enter the room at reference point **O1** and you'll meet an Octopus who asks to be set free. A Messian will come in and ask what you're doing. Answer that you intend to free the Octopus and the Messian will let you go. Talk to the Octopus again and instead of eating you, he will give you the Octopus Whistle, which allows transport over water.

This is another area where you'll run into trouble with the translation patch. If you have the patch installed, you'll never be able to save the Octopus, since the game will always think you're answering "No" to the Messian's question. Load an unpatched ROM and talk to the Messian. Answer "yes" to the man's question (the top choice if you're getting gibberish), and continue through the door to pick up the Octopus Whistle. Now you can load up the patched ROM.

###### Chaos Path

While in the northern portion of the Cathedral (the Gaian Encampment), head towards reference point **O2** and you'll meet a Gaian who wants to kill a monster and sell its meat. Enter the room at reference point **O2** and you'll see a turtle that has been captured. The Gaian will enter the room, but he'll quickly notice your awesome weaponry and will decide to let you have the Turtle. Talk to the Turtle, who then gives you the Turtleshell Bell, which allows you to travel over water.

###### Neutral Path

If you are on the Neutral Path, you can do either of the two options above. If you are using the translation patch, then I would recommend rescuing the Turtle to avoid the Octopus glitch entirely.

The Neutral path entrance to the Cathedral is on Basement One, however, as opposed to the First Floor that the other two paths start at. As such, you will want to first make your way to the First Floor. ###### Chaos Path

Your next objective is to reach the bottom of the Cathedral so that you can meet with the Chaos Hero and King Asura. As such, you will be going into the underground portion of the Cathedral before climbing to the top. Pick up the walkthrough at the [The Great Cathedral - To the Cathedral Depths](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/snes/564896-shin-megami-tensei/faqs/52976?page=4#section496) section below.

###### Neutral Path

Since your objective is to neutralize both Seraph Michael and Tenma King Asura, you can choose whether to tackle the Cathedral basement first, or to head to the top. Since the path to the top has slightly more difficult bosses, and because you can do an extra sidequest without any backtracking by doing the basement first, I recommend heading to the bottom first.

If you choose to go to the basement first, pick up the walkthrough at the [The Great Cathedral - To the Cathedral Depths](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/snes/564896-shin-megami-tensei/faqs/52976?page=4#section496) section below.

If you choose to go to the cathedral top first, pick up the walkthrough at the [The Great Cathedral - To the Top of the Cathedral](https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/snes/564896-shin-megami-tensei/faqs/52976?page=4#section435) section below.
